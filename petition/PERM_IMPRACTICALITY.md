# PERM impracticality (one page)

Labor certification exists to protect U.S. workers for *occupations* that
O\*NET can describe. The minimum PERM job that a reasonable employer would
post for this work would read approximately:

> Software engineer, 2 years experience, Python, machine learning.

That posting under-specifies the person and the work. The actual endeavor is
a CET-aligned research program that combines, in one role:

1. telephony DSP and channel-native evaluation (not generic ML ops);
2. streaming discourse / social-engineering trajectory under ASR error;
3. sequential decision under a legally constrained interruption budget;
4. production systems doctrine for a fail-open sidecar beside RTP.

A PERM advertisement written at the O\*NET “software developer” grain would
either (a) fail to attract the combination, or (b) be so generic that it
cannot be certified against the actual duties without misrepresentation.

The national interest at issue is not “another Python engineer.” It is
whether the United States obtains this dual-stream, mid-call,
privacy-preserving telephony program on a timeline faster than PERM.
Urgency is documented separately (`URGENCY.md`).
