# Urgency memo (one page)

AI-voice impersonation on the PSTN is moving faster than labor-certification
timelines.

- FCC has already classified AI-generated voices as artificial under the TCPA
  (8 February 2024). That is a live regulatory clock, not a future market.
- FTC Voice Cloning Challenge (2024) treated detection and prevention as a
  present consumer-protection R&D need.
- Elder-fraud and government-impersonation losses reported through Sentinel
  have grown, not shrunk, in the last reporting cycles. Victims do not wait
  for PERM.
