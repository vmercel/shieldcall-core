# Occupation versus endeavor

Pattern from *Matter of Dhanasar*, 26 I&N Dec. 884 (AAO 2016): occupation is
the job family; endeavor is the specific research-and-systems program.

**Occupation.** Research scientist in speech signal processing and telephony
security (a profession for which a bachelor’s degree in CS, EE, or a closely
related field is the normal entry requirement).

**Endeavor.** Invent and field a telephony-native, dual-stream, closed-loop
defense against AI-enabled voice fraud on U.S. telephone networks — the
paragraph in `ENDEAVOR.md`, not “work as a software engineer” and not
“consult on AI.”

The occupation is how the work is staffed. The endeavor is what the petition
asks the United States to waive labor certification *for*.
