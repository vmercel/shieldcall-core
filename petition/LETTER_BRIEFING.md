# Letter-writer briefing packet

Send this, or they will write slop.

1. Two-page plain-language description: `ENDEAVOR.md` + the abstract of
   `paper/main.pdf`.
2. The paper PDF.
3. Three things to evaluate **independently** (they must sound like themselves):
   - Is the **disjunctive** telephony threat (script OR production, including
     mid-call handoff) a real gap relative to ASVspoof and keyword products?
   - Are the **negative results** (LPC at chance; SDTG ≉ wide lexicon on
     independent text; SAPC audio not supported) handled honestly?
   - Is a sufficient-statistic agent that **refuses to nonce-challenge a
     live social engineer** a sensible control policy?
4. Explicit: “Please do not write about visa eligibility.”
5. Ask them to state **how they know the work** (read the paper; ran
   `pytest` / `run_upgrade_experiments.py`; saw a trial).
6. Attach the writer’s CV.

Do not recruit relatives, equity holders, or anyone who has not seen the
work.
