## What the audio subset contains

Seeded draw (seed 20261107), stratified by official split and attack id:

| Split | Role | Speakers | Genuine | Spoof | Attacks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| train | fit models | 20 | 2,580 | 2,580 | A01-A06 (430 each) |
| dev | thresholds, calibration, abstention band | 20 | 1,000 | 1,020 | A01-A06 (170 each) |
| test (held out) | scored only | 67 | 1,000 | 1,300 | A07-A19 (100 each) |

* **Speaker-disjoint**: zero overlap between train, dev and test (asserted in the build script).
* **Attack-family split**: test attacks A07-A19 are different spoofing systems from the training attacks, except A16 and A19, whose README descriptions match A04 and A06. Attack types are in `docs/results/BENCHMARK_PHASE1_2.md`.
* **Silence trimmed**: leading and trailing frames below -40 dB of the loudest frame are cut before any channel is applied. ASVspoof's silence duration is a documented shortcut; we remove it.
* **Language**: English only (VCTK). The language axis is therefore **not covered** for audio.

## Text subset

800 dev / 800 test dialogues, seeded 50/50 split stratified by dialogue type (`data/manifests/scam_dialogue_manifest.csv`). Scam types: ssn, refund, support, reward. Benign types: delivery, insurance, telemarketing, wrong number. Because every dialogue is LLM-written, results on it are **not evidence about real scam calls**; a TF-IDF baseline separates the classes perfectly, which signals a stylistic artifact of the generator.

## Channel conditions (applied to test clips after trimming)

REAL codecs through FFmpeg (PyAV): G.711 u-law, G.711 A-law, AMR-NB 12.2 and 4.75 kbit/s, Opus narrowband 12 kbit/s, Opus wideband 24 kbit/s. Plus additive white noise at 20/10/0 dB SNR on u-law, 10% packet loss on u-law, a numpy 300-3400 Hz band-limit, and two numpy simulations (`HARSH_VOIP`, `DEGRADED_PSTN` from `audio/channel.py`). Not covered: AMR-WB, EVS, G.729, real network jitter.

## Limits

Public research corpora, studio-quality source recordings, 2019-era attacks, English only, synthetic text. Nothing here shows performance on real calls or modern voice-cloning systems.
